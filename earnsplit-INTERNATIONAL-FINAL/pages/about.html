How EarnSplit 70/30 Works

Built by VortexForge Studios for international users including Nigeria.

1. Where does 30% go?

When you complete an offer, advertiser pays BitLabs $2.50. BitLabs owes YOU $2.50. Your code splits: You get $1.75 (70%), VortexForge Studios keeps $0.75 (30%). That $0.75 sits in BitLabs dashboard. BitLabs pays VortexForge weekly via Wise/Geegpay USD account, PayPal, or USDT. You do NOT need Paystack to receive your 30%. Paystack is only needed to collect Pro membership fees from NG users and to pay NG users to their bank.

2. 10 Networks + 3 Ad Layers

We aggregate BitLabs, Timewall, Cpx Research, Primewall, Adscend, AdGate, Lootably, Inbrain, Impact.com, Digistore24. Plus Monetag Rewarded $15 CPM, Adsterra Native $4 CPM, Push notifications. Total 500+ offers, so pool is always full.

3. Built for Millions

Wallet uses UNIQUE transaction ID + Redis lock to prevent double pay when 10k users click at once. 7-day pending prevents fraud. All transactions append-only. Supabase with read replicas for 1M+ users.

4. International + Nigeria

US offers pay $1-$65, NG offers pay $0.5-$5. Geo-targeted. Payouts: PayPal/Wise/USDT for international, Paystack bank transfer for NG. Min $10 USD / ₦5000.
